Silvora vs Google Drive

Google Drive is a brilliant product, and for most things it's the right choice. This page is about the one thing it isn't built to do: keep your files private from the company storing them.

The quick answer

Which one is right for you?

Google Drive, if you…

Edit documents with other people, want lots of free space, use a computer and an iPhone as much as Android, and are comfortable with Google being able to process your files.

Silvora, if you…

Want a place for files nobody else should read, not even the company storing them: ID scans, contracts, medical records, client work, personal photos.

Being fair

Where Google Drive is better.

  • Features. Documents, spreadsheets, comments, real-time editing, search inside PDFs and photos. Silvora does none of that, because it can't read your files.
  • Free space. 15 GB free against Silvora's 1 GB.
  • Everywhere. Drive works on the web, on iPhone and on your computer. Silvora is an Android app today.
  • Forgiving. Forget your Google password and Google can get you back in. Silvora can't, by design.
  • Track record. Google has run Drive for over a decade. Silvora is new.
Where Silvora is different

Private from us, too.

  • A breach exposes nothing readable. If Silvora's servers were broken into, the attacker would get locked data and no keys.
  • Nothing to hand over. We can't be made to decrypt your files, because we can't decrypt them.
  • Filenames stay private. "Passport scan.pdf" says a lot on its own. Silvora encrypts names as well as contents.
  • No ads, no tracking inside the app. Paying is the whole business model.

The details are on the security page.

What I'd suggest

Use both.

You don't have to choose. Keep everyday documents, shared folders and anything you edit with others in Google Drive. Put the files you'd hate to see leaked in Silvora: Aadhaar and PAN scans, passport copies, property papers, contracts, medical reports, salary slips, work you haven't released yet.

The free 1 GB is enough for years of ID scans and important PDFs.

Questions

People usually ask.

Isn't Google Drive already encrypted?

Yes, Drive encrypts files while they travel and while they're stored. But Google holds the keys, which is how it can show previews, search inside documents and check files for malware. With Silvora, only your phone holds the keys.

Should I stop using Google Drive?

Probably not. Drive is excellent for everyday documents and working with other people. Use Silvora for the files you'd be upset to see leaked: ID scans, contracts, medical records, unreleased work.

How do I move files from Google Drive to Silvora?

Download them from Drive to your phone, or export everything with Google Takeout, then upload them in the Silvora app. They're encrypted on your phone as they go up.

Is Silvora as reliable as Google?

Silvora is much newer and much smaller than Google. For anything you truly can't lose, keep a second copy somewhere else. That's good advice for any cloud service, including Drive.
